# Easy Growth Planner

**Compare, Play and Grow!** A compound interest planner that runs in the browser. It needs no install and no build step.

**Live app:** https://pearl-vision.github.io/easy-growth-calculator/

## What it does

- Enter a starting amount, a regular contribution (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly), a yearly interest rate and how often interest compounds (daily, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly). Compounding starts on yearly.
- Every number box starts at 0, so you type your own figures. The page starts fresh each time it opens.
- Choose a period of 1 to 50 years, with quick buttons for 10, 20, 30 and 40 years.
- Add inflation, either one rate for every year or a different rate for each year, to see the final value in today's money.
- See the results in summary tiles, a stacked growth chart and a year-by-year table.

## Compare options

- **Option A** is your main details.
- **Options B and C** each have their own contribution, rate, compounding and inflation (the same as A, or their own rate).
- **Option D** is either **buying a house** with a loan or **investing in shares**:
  - House: price, deposit, buying costs, yearly costs, loan term, house value growth and a loan rate that can stay the same or change each year (the repayment is worked out again when the rate changes). The final value is your equity: the house value minus the loan still owed.
  - Shares: starting amount, contribution, expected return and compounding. **Use the same money as the house** puts in exactly the same total as the house.
  - Option D can use Option A's inflation or its own.
- When you compare, the top card shows the **winner** (the biggest final value). The comparison shows a summary table, a chart you can switch between lines, bars, shaded areas and the difference from Option A, and year-by-year balances. The main year-by-year table adds columns for each option.

## Currencies and rates

- Pick from 9 currencies, or type any other currency. The page guesses a starting currency from the device's time zone or region. It reads these from the device and sends nothing anywhere.
- Browse advertised savings and term deposit rates for the USA, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, the UK, Europe, India, Singapore and Japan, checked in November 2026. Click **Use this rate** to load one into Option A, B or C.
- For other currencies, use the research links to find local rates and save them. Saved rates stay in that browser on that device only.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in any browser.

## Disclaimer

Advertised rates change often. Check them with the bank before you rely on them. Tax and fees are not included. House prices and share returns are steady yearly guesses; real ones go up and down, and rent is not included. This is for learning only and is not financial advice. Everything here is speculative and subject to change.
