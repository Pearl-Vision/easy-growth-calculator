# Easy Growth Calculator

A compound interest calculator that runs in the browser. It needs no install and no build step.

**Live app:** https://pearl-vision.github.io/easy-growth-calculator/

## What it does

- Enter a starting amount, a regular contribution (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly), a yearly interest rate and how often interest compounds (daily, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly).
- Choose a period of 1 to 50 years, with quick buttons for 10, 20, 30 and 40 years.
- Add inflation, either one rate for every year or a different rate for each year, to see the final value in today's money.
- See the results in summary tiles, a stacked growth chart and a year-by-year table.
- Pick from 9 currencies, or type any other currency. The page guesses a starting currency from the device's time zone or region. It reads these from the device and sends nothing anywhere.
- Browse advertised savings and term deposit rates for the USA, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, the UK, Europe, India, Singapore and Japan, checked in November 2026. Click **Use this rate** to load one into the calculator.
- For other currencies, use the research links to find local rates and save them. Saved rates stay in that browser on that device only.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in any browser.

## Disclaimer

Advertised rates change often. Check them with the bank before you rely on them. Tax and fees are not included. This is for learning only and is not financial advice.
